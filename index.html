1968Origins

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename the newly formed company NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

Why it matters

Every long-term commitment starts with a clear purpose and a willingness to build for the future.

1971Innovation

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, igniting the microprocessor revolution and propelling the future of computing devices.

Why it matters

Smaller, more efficient computing opened the door to technology that could reach more people.

1978Architecture

8086 Processor

Launch of the 8086 processor, establishing the x86 architecture that drives countless PCs and servers in the modern era.

Why it matters

Innovation has impact when it becomes a platform others can build on.

1985Performance

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, ushering in a new era of performance and multitasking for personal computers.

Why it matters

Better performance helps more work happen with the same device and infrastructure.

2006Turning point

Peak GHG Emissions

This year marks Intel's highest annual greenhouse gas emissions for operations. Over subsequent years, Intel invests heavily in chemical abatement, renewable energy, and energy-efficient manufacturing to reverse this trend.

See the shift

Measuring the challenge made it possible to focus investment where it could have the greatest effect.

20202030 goals

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its RISE (Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling) strategy and 2030 goals, aiming to drive industry-wide progress on climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

See the focus

RISE connects environmental action with inclusion, responsible business, and the communities Intel serves.

2022North star

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel announces its commitment to achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and 2) across its global operations by 2040, building on years of environmental initiatives.

See the commitment

A clear deadline turns a broad ambition into a measurable direction for decisions today.

2023Progress

Renewable Electricity

The company achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, helping to drastically lower carbon emissions and driving progress toward Intel's long-term sustainability goals.

See the progress

Renewable electricity is one practical way to reduce operational emissions at a global scale.

2024Together

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit, uniting suppliers, government officials, and industry leaders to collaborate on next-generation sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.

See the next step

Progress accelerates when companies share ideas, expectations, and responsibility across the whole industry.

Intel sustainability journey / 1968—2040
Climate Action

Intel is targeting net-zero greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and 2) across global operations by 2040, backed by ongoing investment in renewable energy, chemical abatement, and energy-efficient manufacturing.

Since 2023, the company has sourced 99% of its electricity worldwide from renewable sources.

Water Stewardship

Water conservation, reuse, and restoration are core to Intel's manufacturing footprint, with ongoing partnerships aimed at giving back more water than the company consumes in water-stressed regions.

Shared Progress

Intel's 2024 Sustainability Summit brought together suppliers, government officials, and industry leaders to align on next-generation sustainable semiconductor manufacturing practices.
